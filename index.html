Practical Tips

  • Drive less: combine trips, carpool, or use public transport.
  • Shift to lower-carbon electricity where possible or improve home efficiency.
  • Reduce red-meat consumption — try a few plant-based meals weekly.
  • Choose fewer short flights; consider trains for regional travel.
  • Offset or support verified climate projects for unavoidable emissions.
