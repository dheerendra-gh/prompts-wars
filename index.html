Practical Tips

  • Travel: Combine trips, use public transport, or try carpooling.
  • Home energy: Improve insulation and switch to low-carbon tariffs.
  • Diet: Replace some meat meals with plant-based alternatives.
  • Flights: Prefer trains for regional travel when practical.
  • Offset: Consider verified carbon offset projects for unavoidable trips.
